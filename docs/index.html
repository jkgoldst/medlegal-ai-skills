Your medical-legal judgment matters more than ever.

MedLegal AI Hero is the practical process for using AI in medical-legal evaluations, from record review to a finished, defensible work product.

Browse the skills

You shouldn't have to trust AI blindly.

A lot of physicians and other medical-legal experts are skeptical of AI. For good reason. AI can miss important facts, misread records, and produce confident answers that sound right but aren't.

The answer isn't to hand over your judgment. It's to use AI in a way that keeps the evidence visible, the reasoning reviewable, and the expert in control.

Used well, AI can help you review more thoroughly, find what matters faster, and spend more of your time on the clinical reasoning and professional judgment only you can provide.

MedLegal AI Hero is for experts who want the benefits of AI without having to take its answers on faith.

Two ways to use MedLegal AI Hero

Run the Skills for free

The Skills are free to use in the AI account you already have. There's no MedLegal AI Hero login, and your case data does not come to us.

You stay in control of the environment you choose. Our environment check helps you understand whether that account is appropriate for sensitive medical-legal information before you use it on a real case.

Or use Tresa for a purpose-built medical-legal workspace

Tresa is a secure environment designed specifically for medical-legal cases.

Instead of working with one document or task at a time, Tresa keeps the case together: records, interview, findings, evidence, and report. It can check new work against the rest of the case and carry that context forward throughout the evaluation.

The free Skills help you use AI well. Tresa gives you a secure place to do the whole case.
