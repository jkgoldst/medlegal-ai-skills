You shouldn't have to trust AI blindly.

A lot of physicians are skeptical of AI in medical-legal work. For good reason. AI can miss important facts, misread records, and produce confident answers that sound right but aren't.

The answer isn't to hand over your judgment. It's to use AI in a way that keeps the evidence visible, the reasoning reviewable, and the physician in control.

Used well, AI can help you review more thoroughly, find what matters faster, and spend more of your time on the clinical reasoning only you can provide.

QME AI Hero is for evaluators who want the benefits of AI without having to take its answers on faith.

Runs in your account. Nothing leaves it.

Each tool runs inside the AI account you already pay for. Nothing is uploaded to us, there is no login, and no case data reaches our side. Whether that account is fit for case data is exactly what the environment check tells you.

The Skills check what's in front of you. Tresa checks it against everything else in the case.
