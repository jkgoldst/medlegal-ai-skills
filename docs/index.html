Practical AI for medical-legal work

Learn to use AI in medical-legal work. On your own terms.

We publish free, open-source AI skills and practical guides for physicians doing medical-legal work. Use them with compatible AI tools or build workflows of your own.

Our goal is to help you become a confident, independent user of AI: understanding what it does well, what requires your judgment, and how to protect sensitive medical information.

Hi, I'm Josh Goldstein

I've spent much of my career building technology for people doing complicated work in highly regulated environments, particularly in government.

More recently, I founded Tresa, an AI platform for medical-legal evaluations. Working alongside physicians has given me an appreciation for the complexity of their work, the importance of independent medical judgment, and the opportunities and limitations of AI.

I believe medical-legal professionals should have the opportunity to understand, use, and even build their own AI tools, rather than depend entirely on software vendors.

That's why I'm building this free, open-source resource.
